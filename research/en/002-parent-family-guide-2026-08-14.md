## Publishable Angle

- Core thesis: Parents have always helped their children marry well by working their own network, friends, colleagues, social and religious gatherings, siblings, religious leaders, professional matchmakers. Panosia Connect doesn't ask a parent to do something new; it gathers that same trusted network onto one platform, the way LinkedIn or Facebook did for professional and social circles, so a parent can search within it, start real conversations, and vouch for people they know (and be vouched for in return). Because everyone belongs to the same trusted network, a parent who helps their own child ends up able to help others too.
- Reader promise: A clear picture of how to bring your natural way of helping your child onto one trusted platform, without losing the personal, relationship-based feel or taking over your child's choice.
