## Product grounding used (from context/site-profile.md, PRD-04, PRD-05, PRD-09)

- **Reference mechanic**: a Candidate lists someone as a reference on their own profile; the reference does not add themselves. Adapted from the brief's quote-worthy phrasing ("A Candidate can list you as a reference the same way they'd tell a friend, 'ask [name], she knows my family.' Your reference shows up as real context on their profile, not an anonymous claim.").
- Adding a reference, and being listed as one, is free and does not require ID verification — same as profile creation, sharing, and export. This is distinct from search, Discover, messaging, and private access requests, which do require Level 1 ID Verification (PRD-05).
- **Family Tree relationship-tag voting** (PRD-09) is used as a deliberately distinct, contrasting mechanic: anyone with private access to a profile can upvote or downvote a specific claimed relationship tag (father, sister, cousin, etc.), shown as a running +N/-N score. This is community verification of a claimed *relationship*, not one person vouching for the Candidate's *character* — the article keeps this distinction explicit rather than conflating the two mechanics.
- No mechanic beyond what's documented in site-profile.md's Trust & Verification, Private Access Requests, and Social Map/Family Tree sections is asserted as fact.
