Most communities already have an informal matchmaker: someone who's spent years building a reputation for careful, discreet introductions. No fee changes hands. Trust is the entire currency.

Panosia Connect's Connector role gives that instinct one place to work from: a trusted network instead of scattered phone calls and a notebook, a way to ask before sharing private details, and even a way to build a full profile for someone who isn't ready to create one themselves. It doesn't turn goodwill into a paid service. It just makes it easier to keep doing well as it grows.
