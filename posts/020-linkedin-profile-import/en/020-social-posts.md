A LinkedIn profile and a marriage profile answer different questions. One tells a recruiter what you've done. The other tells a family who you are.

It leaves the rest blank on purpose, religion, marital status, family background, because LinkedIn was never built to ask.

Every imported field keeps the same field-level privacy control as anything typed by hand, Public, Privately Shared, or Hidden, and nothing goes live until it's reviewed.
