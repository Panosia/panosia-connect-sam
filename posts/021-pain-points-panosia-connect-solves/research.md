## Candidate pain-point list (10), each mapped to a named Panosia Connect mechanic from `context/site-profile.md`

1. "I can't tell if this person is even real." → Level 1 ID Verification (2-minute self-service or video-call path), locked identity fields on approval, downloadable verification report.
2. "Everyone wants a subscription just to let me look around." → No-subscription model: profile creation, browsing, privacy controls, and messaging (once verified) are all free; only the optional one-time verification services cost money.
3. "My family wants to be part of this, but there's no good way to include them without them taking over." → Connector role (a toggle on the same account type, not a separate tier or takeover) and Managed Profiles for a relative not ready to be online themselves.
4. "I don't want every stranger who finds my profile to see everything about me." → Field-level privacy (Public / Privately Shared / Hidden per field and per photo), the Blurred/Public photo option.
5. "Once I share something private, I can't take it back." → Private Access Requests: approve/decline from a dashboard, access revocable anytime, a revoked request can be re-sent later.
6. "It feels like a swipe app wearing a marriage label." → Discovery centers on networks (Connections, one-hop network browsing) plus a transparent compatibility-score formula (age closeness, location, education, religion) — not an anonymous swipe feed or opaque AI matching.
7. "Anyone can claim to be someone's brother or cousin, and there's no way to check." → Family Tree relationship tags with community upvote/downvote on each specific claimed relationship.
8. "I already have a biodata file from somewhere else and don't want to retype everything." → Biodata Import (AI-assisted, from a PDF/DOC/scanned document) and Biodata Export with a chosen privacy level per copy.
9. "I get messages from people who could be anyone." → Messaging (Chat and Email) gated on the sender holding approved ID verification, plus a Report button built into every thread.
10. "My parent or older relative isn't ready to manage a profile themselves, but still wants to be found." → Managed Profiles: a verified relative creates and fully manages a profile on someone else's behalf, submitted through the same verification review queue.
