## Reader

An Imam, community leader, or Islamic center administrator/volunteer wondering whether or how their institution could get involved in helping congregants find a spouse, without turning the mosque into a matchmaking business.

## Core thesis

A mosque, Islamic center, or community organization is already a trusted hub where families informally ask each other for introductions. Panosia Connect's Connector role, its referral/reputation tiers (Connector → Regional Connector at 10 referrals → Community Leader at 50 referrals), and its verification-reference model give that hub a way to formalize and scale what it already does, without taking on legal or reputational liability for making matches itself.

## Angle

Practical, low-commitment ways a community hub can participate:
- Individual staff/volunteers using the Connector role on their own accounts (not an institutional account — Panosia Connect has no separate "organization" account type).
- Being a References source congregants can list (Candidate-initiated, per the References mechanic).
- Hosting or endorsing the platform to the congregation (a low-liability alternative to running an in-house matchmaking program).
- The Community Leader recognition tier (50 referrals) as a way an especially active community figure's role gets visible platform recognition.

## Avoid

- Overlap with SL#003 (an individual volunteer/community matchmaker as a person) — this piece is about an institution/hub's options, not one person's story. SL#003 is not yet published, so it is not referenced or linked here.
- Implying Panosia Connect has an institutional/organization account type — it does not; any staff/volunteer participation is through their own personal Connector account.
- Implying verification or liability transfers to the institution — the platform's verification and moderation stay member-level (per `context/site-profile.md`'s Trust & Verification and Messaging sections).
