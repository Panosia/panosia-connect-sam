A bride-viewing (or groom-viewing) meeting is still a normal, respected part of how many South Asian and Muslim families arrange a marriage. It isn't an interview, and it isn't just a formality either.

It's the moment two families actually sit down together, after all the phone calls and the paperwork, before anyone commits.

A few questions worth having ready for that meeting:

🕌 What does daily life actually look like: work, living situation, whether that changes after marriage?
🏡 What are the expectations around living with parents versus independently?
🙏 What matters most in day-to-day religious practice?
👪 How does each family typically make big decisions, and who's usually part of that?
