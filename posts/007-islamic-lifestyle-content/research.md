## Editorial Decisions Made During This Drafting Run (2026-09-15)

- **Ramadan timing check**: Ramadan 2026 ran February 18 – March 20, 2026 (confirmed via WebSearch). Today is September 15, 2026, roughly six months past and five months before the next Ramadan (~February 2027). Per the brief's own caution ("if Ramadan timing has passed for the year, pick a different evergreen Islamic-lifestyle angle"), this draft does **not** frame itself as a Ramadan-seasonal piece. It uses an evergreen marriage-readiness/character angle instead, with Ramadan-style reflection mentioned only as one relatable illustrative example, not the hook or the title.
- **Confidence check (per the routine's Step 3 instructions)**: this brief was flagged as the queue's "most exploratory item" with no OpenSEO-verified primary keyword (Notion's Primary Keyword field says "TBD"). A WebSearch sanity check confirmed the general premise is real: existing sites (islamqa.info, aboutislam.net, balancedbayt.com, ayeina.com) already publish and rank for marriage-readiness/spouse-selection content grounded in Islamic teaching, so the topic area itself has genuine reader interest. That WebSearch is not a substitute for OpenSEO keyword-volume validation, though — this topic should still get a real OpenSEO pass (via `/hv-content-planning`) before being treated as equally evidence-backed as the fully keyword-verified rows in the queue.
- **Scope gap, same pattern as SL#005 and SL#006**: the keyword-validated opportunity mapped to this SL# is the Bengali devotional-content cluster above (~2,240/mo combined), not this English piece. This English draft only covers the evergreen marriage-readiness angle for English readers; it does not target that Bengali demand. A separate bn-first piece (via `bn-translator` or a manual session, written to lead naturally with the Bengali terms rather than translated from this English draft) is still needed to actually capture that opportunity.
- **Religious-content care**: per the brief's own caution about brand-voice fit on faith content, this draft avoids citing specific Quran verses or Hadith by number/wording (a real risk of inaccuracy, and this SERP is dominated by dedicated religious-reference sites better positioned to compete on citation precision). It sticks to widely-known, uncontroversial themes (character over wealth/status, patience and self-improvement, family involvement) framed as commonly-taught Islamic values rather than as specific scriptural citations.
